# LinkedIn companion post

Include a separate ready-to-copy LinkedIn caption on every report, after the Facebook caption and before both infographics. Use the same reconciled facts and attribution rules as [the Facebook guide](facebook-post.md), with the platform-specific direction below. Current explicit requests override these defaults. Preparing a caption does not authorize publishing or messaging.

## Approved direction

- Write in the owner's clear, first-person Egyptian Arabic: slightly more structured than Facebook, still personal and accessible. Keep familiar English product names and Western digits (0–9). Another user's language and preferences override the original owner's voice.
- Open with a varied reflection on how Agentic AI helped advance multiple projects or types of work that day. Then substantiate it with actual results. Avoid a stock slogan, unsupported productivity multiplier or repeating the exact same opening every day.
- Follow with a short transition such as «وده جزء من شغل امبارح:», adjusting the day reference when reporting another date. Present achievements as short, spaced paragraphs, each starting with the same RTL-friendly left arrow **⬅️**, then a useful category label and concrete outcome. Use very few other emojis; do not assign a different pictogram to every category. For English posts, use the corresponding LTR direction instead.
- Explain a new product's purpose in one plain sentence. Preserve major creation/revision/delivery stages and useful output counts. Do not turn the post into a test log or use recorded tokens as a productivity boast. Keep scheduled content distinct from actual sending/publication.
- Optionally add a brief personal reflection on the user's documented role in specifying, reviewing, testing and deciding next steps. Do not invent feelings, sleep, effort or hours worked from the chat timestamps.
- Add a simple infographic bridge, such as «الإنفوجرافيك المرفق بيعرض المسارات دي والنتائج اللي وصلنا لها.»
- End with a direct-message CTA for the skill, using the exact configured keyword. Keep LinkedIn's action distinct from Facebook Page delivery; do not promise automatic LinkedIn replies without evidence that such a system exists. Do not replace the CTA with a generic engagement question.
- Finish with a few relevant hashtags, typically three or four. Aim for a readable medium-length post, roughly 200–300 Arabic words on a busy day; shorten quiet days and preserve useful context rather than filling a quota.

The paragraph introducing the newly created daily-report skill and its 4 AM schedule belongs only to an explicitly requested first announcement. Ordinary daily LinkedIn captions retain the AI-productivity opening and skill CTA without repeating the launch explanation. Do not copy one day's achievements, personal anecdotes or third-party project history into future daily posts.

Deliver the caption as one copyable block with natural paragraph breaks. The copy itself should contain no report headings, source footnotes, local file paths, editorial commentary or Markdown emphasis markers. Public project links may appear when requested; the approved keyword/DM format does not require a repository link in the main caption.

Before delivery, check the names, counts, dates, status and provenance against the report, Facebook caption and both images. The original owner's cross-agent attribution corrections remain in the private installed profile, not the portable guide.
