# Mandatory final review

Review the finished report, captions and both actual images together before delivery. Collector success and image-file creation are intermediate evidence, not acceptance.

- Reconcile substantive date-active projects against the inspected source index. Confirm the local calendar date, cutoff, exact or qualified milestone times and source attribution. Preserve meaningful research, writing, editing and revision work instead of reducing a project to its last publication step. Explain inaccessible sources without claiming universal coverage.
- Trace every highlighted outcome to dated evidence and its final state in that day's window. A request, plan, agent-reported success, directly verified result and scheduled post must retain their different meanings. Check reversals and avoid crediting the agent for pre-existing work.
- Reconcile semantic project grouping, deduplicated messages and recorded tokens with the metric definitions and per-agent breakdowns. Do not count chats as projects, add cache/reasoning subsets twice, infer work hours from gaps or silently combine partial coverage into a complete total.
- Read the report and captions as a person discovering the day's work: clear outcomes, readable time blocks, enough context to understand unfamiliar projects, the configured voice and CTA. Check platform-specific copy and ensure captions do not promise publication or automatic delivery that has not been established.
- Open and inspect each generated infographic at a comfortable reading size. Compare every date, time, count, label and completion claim with the reconciled manifest and written report, then compare English with Arabic. Check Arabic shaping/RTL, mixed-language labels, whitespace, hierarchy and the approved brand. Tiny correct text still fails readability.
- Images must show achieved outcomes and delivered progress only, without unfinished-work sections or misleading completion claims. Keep blockers and next steps in the written report. Check the final shareable captions and image pixels/metadata for private details; access to source chats does not authorize public disclosure.

Correct factual or presentation problems and recheck affected outputs; an image edit requires another pixel/text check. Use a short private reconciliation note to retain reviewed outputs, evidence and real limitations. Deliver only inspected outputs, explicitly identifying missing images or unresolved coverage. Do not generate a new report, publish captions or continue the underlying projects merely to complete this review.
